A clear process, from start to finish.

We agree on the work first. Then we deliver it and walk you through the results.

  1. Tell us what you need. Describe the problem, your current systems, and any deadlines.
  2. Agree on scope and cost. We confirm what is included, what information we need, and the schedule in writing.
  3. Do the work. We review records, test systems, or build the agreed solution. We discuss important findings with you.
  4. Hand over the results. You receive the report or system, relevant documentation, and a walkthrough.

You decide what happens next.

An operating review does not commit you to implementation. For a build, we agree on testing and handover before we start.

We agree on access, storage, and retention before you share documents. Full data-handling commitments and commercial terms are available below.
