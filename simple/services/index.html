Build and improve AI systems

We build private AI installations, GPU clusters, and production AI factories. We also investigate systems that are slow or unreliable.

  • Plan the system and choose the right tools.
  • Set up servers, networking, storage, and job scheduling.
  • Test performance and hand the system over to your team.
  • Find problems in networking, storage, and workload scheduling.

Hardware and other project costs are separate where stated. Bounded diagnostics can be booked after a brief qualification call; they do not require a paid architecture assessment.

Find out where revenue is missed, costs are rising, or work is getting stuck. Start with one question, a broader diagnostic, or a detailed review.

AI policy and governance

A policy sets the rules for using AI. Governance assigns responsibility and keeps those rules working as tools and teams change.

  • Identify AI tools already in use.
  • Define what data people may share.
  • Set approval and incident procedures.
  • Keep records for management and client questions.
