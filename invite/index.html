A personal invitation

What it actually is

A full read of how an operation earns and where it leaks — finance, revenue, receivables, payables, contracts, people and IT — with every finding sized in annual value and the evidence attached.

Not a sample. Concentrated loss is exactly what sampling misses: one contract, one queue, one account nobody reviews. A sample of the operation tends to miss those and a full pass tends to find them.

Getting started, if you want to

  1. Read the proposal for your sector It sets out the sequence week by week, what we would need from you, and what it costs for your number of operating units. No call required to see the number.
  2. Send it to whoever owns the numbers Usually the CFO or finance director. The page is written to be forwarded without explanation.
  3. Commission it, or ask first Purchase order is the normal route — most buyers this size raise a PO and pay against an invoice on terms. Your invitation applies either way.
