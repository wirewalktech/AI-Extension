Retail and e-commerce

The margin you report and the margin you keep.

Gross margin is calculated at the point of sale and eroded everywhere afterwards — discount, markdown, returns, fulfilment, platform fees. Realised margin by SKU is a different ranking from reported margin, and often a surprising one.

The terms above are the ones the review uses with you, because they are the ones you already use. Nothing is translated into a consultant's vocabulary and then translated back.

Where it concentrates here

Five losses that recur in retail and e-commerce and are worth going looking for. Each is concentrated — a single agreement, a single queue, a single account nobody reviews — which is precisely why a sample of the operation tends to miss them and a full pass tends to find them. They are not findings from a named engagement; they are what a review of retail and e-commerce is built to look for.

  1. Returns cost — processing, refurbishment, disposal — carried centrally and never allocated to the category generating it.
  2. Marketplace and platform fees, including advertising, taking a share that is not in the product margin calculation.
  3. Markdown applied to clear stock that was over-bought, with no feedback into the next buying decision.
  4. Shrinkage concentrated in a small number of sites or SKUs and reported as a company percentage.
  5. Supplier funding and rebates agreed and not fully claimed.

What the review would ask you for

8 further requests, specific to retail and e-commerce — 3 of them core — on top of the 107 the intake asks of every organisation, across finance, revenue, receivables, payables, contracts, people, IT, operations, risk, commercial and governance.
  • Sales by channel, category and month Core
  • Gross margin by SKU and category Core After discount, markdown and returns. Reported margin and realised margin rarely match.
  • Inventory by age and location Core Including obsolete and aged stock.
  • Shrinkage and stock loss By site, with investigation outcomes.
  • Returns and refunds Rate by channel and category, and the cost to process.
  • Fulfilment and last-mile cost Per order, by channel.
  • Marketplace and platform fees Commission, advertising and fulfilment fees.
  • Promotional spend and its measured effect Including supplier funding.
