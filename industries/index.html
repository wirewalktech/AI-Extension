Industries

Your operation, in your own vocabulary.

The argument is the same everywhere: loss concentrates rather than distributes, so a sample of thirty transactions and eight interviews is the wrong instrument for finding it. What differs by sector is where it concentrates, and what it is called.

Below, 20 industries. Each page names five concentrated losses that recur in that sector — not abstractions, and not case studies — and shows what the intake would actually ask for. Find yours, or read the method first if you would rather start there.

Professional and advisory firms

Where the product is time, and three different numbers describe the same hour.

Law firms The gap between recorded, billed and collected. realisation · WIP · leverage · origination credit Professional services Sold at a rate, delivered at another. utilisation · realisation · WIP · write-down Agencies and media The retainer that stopped covering the work. overservice · retainer scope · billability · pass-through Staffing and recruitment Margin per placement, after everything. pay-to-charge · gross margin per placement · rebate · contractor book

Financial services and insurance

Where regulated cost is an operating line, and the operations underneath it are rarely priced.

Financial services Compliance is an operating line, not a department. cost of control · reconciliation break · KYC/AML · remediation Insurance Loss ratio gets the attention. Expense ratio decides it. loss ratio · expense ratio · claims leakage · acquisition cost

Making, moving and building

Where margin is calculated from standards, and the standards drift.

Manufacturing Standard cost said one thing. The line said another. standard cost · yield · scrap and rework · OEE Warehousing and logistics The quoted rate and the paid rate are different numbers. cost per unit handled · accessorials · backhaul · throughput Transport and fleet Cost per mile, and the asset standing still. cost per mile · utilisation · empty running · VOR Construction and contracting Built, and never billed. variation · change order · retention · WIP

Property, energy and infrastructure

Where the asset is the business, and what is contracted is not what is collected.

Real estate Contracted is not the same as collected. rent roll · void · service charge recovery · escalation Energy and utilities Assets that outlive the people who specified them. asset register · planned vs reactive · outage cost · non-technical losses

Selling and serving

Where volume hides the unit, and the unit is where the loss is.

Retail and e-commerce The margin you report and the margin you keep. realised margin · markdown · shrinkage · sell-through Hospitality and food service Two costs decide it, and both move daily. theoretical vs actual · daypart · RevPAR · ADR Technology and SaaS Recurring revenue, and the cost that recurs against it. ARR · net revenue retention · cost to serve · committed use

Public, funded and regulated

Where the funder, the regulator or the statute sets the operating model, and the cost of complying with it is nobody's line item.

Healthcare Denied once, and then never touched again. revenue cycle · denials · reason code · underpayment Government contracting The contract terms are the operating model. indirect rate · provisional billing rate · unallowable cost · flow-down Public sector Statutory duty, and the cost of discharging it. outturn · virement · statutory vs discretionary · unit cost Education and research Funded work, and the cost of administering it. indirect cost recovery · effort reporting · F&A · award closeout Nonprofit and grant-funded Restricted money, and the cost of proving it. restricted funds · cost allocation · programme vs support · reserves policy
