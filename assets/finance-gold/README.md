# Finance gold demo assets

Created for the user-requested financial-services page design demonstration, September 17, 2026.

Gold-coin photographs are AI-generated editorial still lifes, with unbranded coins and no currency denominations or logos. They are decorative, not photographs of client assets, investment products, or actual holdings. No claims about investment returns or services have been added.

`demo.css` is shared by the two gold demos. `horizontal.css` applies only to `/demos/financial-services-gold-horizontal/`. The original `/industries/financial-services/` page remains unchanged. The demo has noindex and is excluded from the sitemap; its canonical points at the original. Service/proposal links remain functional and lead to the existing site.

Keep the original generated source images outside the published folder; use the optimized JPEG exports on the page.

The second demo uses `coins-horizontal.jpg`: a separately generated still life showing two gold-coin rolls lying on their sides, with loose coins on ivory stone. It preserves the finance copy and links to both the first gold demo and original page. It also has noindex, sitemap exclusion and the original-page canonical.
